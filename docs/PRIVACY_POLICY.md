# Draft privacy notice — not for publication yet
Whim Cart Dream is a simulated shopping service, not a real retailer or bank. Planned account data includes email, credentials stored as hashes, virtual orders, balances and optional wellness preferences. Optional telephone confirmations require a verified number and explicit consent. No real payments, delivery addresses, advertising trackers or sale of personal data are permitted by design.
Data is intended for account operation, simulation, security and requested calls. Customers should be able to access, correct, export and delete their data and withdraw call consent. See DATA_PROTECTION.md for proposed retention. Before publication, confirm actual processing, operator identity/contact, applicable jurisdictions, legal bases, providers, transfers, retention, age eligibility and complaint rights. Do not present planned controls as implemented or claim legal compliance.
